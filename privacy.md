# COEWS Smart Home - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

The COEWS Home Assistant app is a private app used only by the two people in one household, to show and manage their shared Google Calendar on their own Home Assistant server. It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses: with your permission, the app uses Google Calendar access to read the events on the calendars of the signed-in Google account, and to create or change events when you ask it to from Home Assistant.

Where data goes: calendar data is sent only to the household's own Home Assistant server on its local network. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or sent to any other service.

Storage: calendar data is kept only in that local Home Assistant server. The Google sign-in token is stored there so Home Assistant can stay connected.

Removing access: you can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account under Security, Third-party apps with account access, or by removing the Google Calendar integration from Home Assistant.

Contact: questions can be sent to the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
