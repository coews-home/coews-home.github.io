# COEWS Smart Home - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

The COEWS Home Assistant app is a private app used only by one household to connect its own Google Nest devices to its own Home Assistant server. It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses: with your permission, the app uses Google's Smart Device Management access to read the status of, and control, the Nest devices on the signed-in Google account, such as thermostat temperature and mode, and camera or doorbell events.

Where data goes: this data is sent only to the household's own Home Assistant server on its local network. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or sent to any other service.

Storage: device data is kept only in that local Home Assistant server. The Google sign-in token is stored there so Home Assistant can stay connected.

Removing access: you can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account under Security, Third-party apps with account access, or by removing the Nest integration from Home Assistant.

Contact: questions can be sent to the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
